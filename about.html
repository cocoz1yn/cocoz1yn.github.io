About

As a geospatial analyst and interdisciplinary planner, I want to understand how cities work and how spatial data can help us make better decisions about their future. What I find most interesting about working with data is not just discovering patterns, but explaining what they mean. The right data visualization can make complex information easier to understand and allow for better engagement

I am also intrigued by how new technologies are changing the way we plan and develop infrastructure. As cities continue to grow and adapt, I see opportunities to use innovative data and new tools to understand what existing systems need and how to improve them. In particular, I am interested in spatial analysis and technology when it comes to finding ways to support future development.

Additionally, I approach urban planning from a legal perspective. This has made me more invested in the connection between policy and the built environment, as well as how those decisions affect public health and how spatial data can help us examine these differences. I hope to keep exploring where these interests overlap and how they can shape how we understand and plan for the future of cities.
